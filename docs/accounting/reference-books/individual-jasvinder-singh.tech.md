## Checks

- `build.py` asserts: every voucher sums to zero with ≥ 2 non-zero lines and no account twice; money in/out touches a money account and no-money entries do not (a Money out may also credit an advance it recovers or the lender's part of a purchase); a transfer joins two own money/deposit accounts; spending from a fund charges the year and moves the same amount out of the fund; the gollak is filled only by a count and emptied only into cash or bank; openings and the trial balance balance; cash, bank and wallet never go negative; advances, loans given, retention, things owned and deposits are never over-settled; fixed capital does not move; every year-close entry balances, the result account and every income, expense and drawings account stand at nil after them, and the own money after them equals the balance sheet's; the position make-up equals the position at both dates; the balance sheet balances; the carry-forward sums to zero; every inter-book pair agrees entry for entry; the entry count is within the compact range; no book prints on more than 14 A4 pages.
- `check_md.py` re-derives the printed book independently (no shared code): chart totals, the Day Book entry by entry, every ledger row by row, the Cash Book with its month totals, the bank-statement extract, both positions and their make-up, the one-month summary, the chosen-period statement, the ageing, the trial balance, the year's statement, the appropriation, the Receipts & Payments, each fund's statement, the year-close entries, the balance sheet, the carry-forward and the family summary, and that every report head carries the book, the date or period, the basis, Year closed and Made on.
