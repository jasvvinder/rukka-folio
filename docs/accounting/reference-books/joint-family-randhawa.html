Joint family and its businesses — the Randhawa family (seven books)

Three brothers — Amarjeet, Lakhbeer and Lakhwinder Randhawa — keep a joint household. Randhawa Joint Family is the joint family's own book: the house, the gold, the deposits, the common kitchen, and one account for each sub-family — what the family spent for that household or gave it, less what the household put in, into the family or into a family business. A sub-family account is not a debt: at the year end it is settled or carried forward, as the family decides. Each brother's household keeps its own book. The family owns Randhawa Agriculture and Randhawa Real Estate outright and shares Randhawa Brick Field 3 : 2 with an outside partner, Nirmaljeet Gill; money a business sends home is the family's income. Money that moves between two books is written in both, in accounts named after the other book, and the family summary shows each pair agreeing.
