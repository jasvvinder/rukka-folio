## Basis (ADR 2026-10-12 §2, §10–§12; ADR 2026-10-20 §2; rulings of 20 Oct 2026 in `docs/archive/reference/accounting-research/OPEN.md`)

- No tax, GST, TDS, invoicing, stock or payroll (`docs/00-vision.md`, non-goals). Purchases for resale are an expense when bought or taken on credit; there is no closing stock.
- Transaction-driven: every entry is a bank or cash movement, credit with a named party, a transfer between own accounts, an advance or its settlement, a partner's own-pocket payment, retention money released, or a correction.
- The khata basis (O-01-1): no depreciation, prepaid, outstanding or accrued entries and no closing stock; things owned stay at cost (O-09-1). The year close is **posted** by the book itself (O-19-1) on 31 Mar 2026 — income and expense heads to the result, the result and Drawings to own money, a partnership through a posted P&L Appropriation Account (O-14-1), a trust's surplus or deficit to the General Fund — listed below as entries `<prefix>-Y1 …`, formal type `Journal (year close)`; none is typed by the user and none is in the Day Book export.
- Plain words in the printed book; the true side stays in a small bracket after the plain label (ADR 2026-10-10b §4). Ledger rows carry each entry's note in small type; the Day Book and the Cash Book are an export (`partnership-bal-fabricators.export.md`), not part of the printed book (ADR 2026-10-12 §11, amended 13 Oct 2026).
