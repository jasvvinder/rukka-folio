# Individual and own business — Jasvinder Singh and Jasvinder Technologies

**Jasvinder Singh** keeps two books. His **personal book** holds the household: a savings account, cash, a credit card, a FASTag wallet, a fixed deposit, two cars (the second bought in October on a car loan that the lender paid straight to the dealer), a hand loan to a friend, a driver's advance, a house help's salary advance and a grocer's khata. **Jasvinder Technologies** is his one-owner IT services business: clients billed on khata, a hosting supplier on credit, one developer on salary. Money moves between the two books only when he puts money into the business or when the business sends money home for the household — each time it is written in both books, in accounts named after the other book. What the business sends home is the household's income; the business is held in the personal book at the money put in. Both books are kept from the bank statement.

## The two books side by side — the books agree

*Jasvinder Singh · Jasvinder Technologies · FY 2025-26 · Kept on money paid and received, and what people owe; no year-end adjustments. · Year closed · Made on 02 Apr 2026, 10:00*

Money that moves between two books is written in both, in accounts named after the other book. For each pair: the balances brought forward agree, and what one book gave is what the other received, entry for entry. A family and a sub-family also agree balance for balance on opposite sides. An owner and the business he owns agree entry by entry — money put in against the business's capital, money sent home against its drawings — but not in balance: the owner holds the business at the money put in, while the business's own money also carries its profit, so the difference is printed under the table as *profit kept in the business*.

**Profit kept in the business — Jasvinder Singh and Jasvinder Technologies:** the business's own money after the year close ₹8,24,080, less the money put in that Jasvinder Singh holds ₹6,79,740, is ₹1,44,340. That is the business's profit for Jasvinder Singh ₹6,24,340 less the ₹4,80,000 taken out (₹6,24,340 − ₹4,80,000 = ₹1,44,340).

The two sides agree entry by entry, on every date. The personal book holds the business at the money put in (₹6,79,740) and takes the money the business sends home as income (*Income from Jasvinder Technologies*, ₹4,80,000 this year); in the business book the same money is the owner's drawings. The business's net profit for the year, ₹6,24,340, is worked out in its own book and is not written in the personal book; after the year close the business's own money is ₹8,24,080.
