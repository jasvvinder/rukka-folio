Trust — Radha Soami Satsang Sabha

A satsang sabha with a gollak counted every two months before the secretary and a witness and emptied only into the cash box or the bank, sponsors' donations by bank, donations for the dairy, a langar run through the sewadar's advance, a small dairy, a new langar hall being built — written as Construction, an expense head — and two labourers, Manni and Ravinder, paid in cash each quarter, each with his own expense head. Daily-wage labour at the big diwans is Labour. Money given for the langar hall goes to the Langar Hall Fund with its own fixed deposit, whose interest is added to the fund; when the hall is paid for from the fund the cost is charged to the year and the same amount is moved out of the fund in one action. The year-end statements are Receipts & Payments, Income & Expenditure, the Balance Sheet with the General Fund, and a statement of each fund.

WhoRole in this example
Ranjeet SinghSecretary — witness at every gollak count; signs the bank payments
Amritpal SinghSewadar — counts the gollak; holds the langar advance and accounts for it
Manni · RavinderLabourers paid in cash each quarter — each has his own expense head
Kotli Cement StoreBuilding-material store — cement and bricks for the hall on khata
Three families and an NRI donorGave for the langar hall — the money went to the Langar Hall Fund; one family donated a steel almirah that the trust keeps

Amounts are in ₹; paise are shown only when there are paise. Plain words come first and the true side follows in small brackets — Money in (Dr), You will give (Cr) — so the side is never lost. Every figure is illustrative.
