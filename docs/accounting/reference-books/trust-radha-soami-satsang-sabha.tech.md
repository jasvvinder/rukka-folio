- No tax, GST, TDS, invoicing, stock or payroll (`docs/00-vision.md`, non-goals). Purchases for resale are an expense when bought or taken on credit; there is no closing stock.
- Transaction-driven: every entry is a bank or cash movement, a khata entry with a named party, a loan instalment as the bank paid it, a transfer between own accounts, an advance or its settlement, a partner's own-pocket payment, or a correction.
