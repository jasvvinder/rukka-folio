01

Principles

Three brand rules corner the design, and the corner is the right place to be.

The mark never loads. The seal-break animation is earned by a real unlock and is never decorative — so the logo cannot be the spinner, and there is no spinner. Every decorative element is a rule, a column, or a fold — so the loader is a rule: a 2px hairline in the rule colour carrying an ink segment. State the number — so whenever progress is known, the loader says what it counted, not a percentage.

A ledger loads the way a ledger fills: line by line. Everything below follows from that.

Every demo on this page has a dark counterpart — use the Dark mode toggle at the bottom right. Dark mode is required, not optional: most users open this app at night after closing shop.

02

The loader rule

One component, two modes. Ink segment on a hairline track — ink, not indigo, because indigo means actionable and a loader is not an affordance.

Motion tokens

TokenValueMeaning

loader-escalate8sPast this, add one line saying what is actually happening.

Behaviour

Determinate whenever the app can count — restore, statement import, month close, export. Copy pattern: “{done} of {total} {things} {verbed}”. Indeterminate only when counting is genuinely impossible, and always with a verb (“Syncing…”, never bare). Announce it: the loader is a live region — “Restoring, 1,240 of 3,890” — an accessible loader is part of the legibility promise.

Flutter note: this is a plain LinearProgressIndicator with minHeight: 2 and token colours — no dependency. Web: a 2px div pair; the CSS in this page's source is the reference.

Dark mode

The loader inverts exactly, keeping its logic: track becomes paper at 16%, segment becomes solid paper — the segment is always the surface's ink, whichever mode. Skeleton blocks become paper at 9% and 14%. Credit and debit keep their hues with lifted lightness (#4FA37A / #C96A6A) so signed amounts stay legible on ink without turning neon. Toggle the page to see all three cards inverted.

03

Skeleton rows

Lists load as ruled skeletons: hairlines at the true row pitch, a muted label block left, a right-aligned amount block. No shimmer — a pulsing gradient is decoration, and it isn't a rule, a column, or a fold.

Rules

Row pitch, hairline weight and block positions match the real ledger row exactly, so content swaps in with zero layout shift. Label blocks vary width (38–58%) so the skeleton doesn't strobe as a grid. Amount blocks are right-aligned and slightly darker — the debit/credit column is present even before the data is. Show at most one screenful of skeleton rows; beyond that, the loader rule with a count is more honest. Skeletons are static in all cases; they are already the reduced-motion variant.

04

The splash screen

Two phases and three branches. Phase 1 is the OS's static frame; Phase 2 is the app's first frame — drawn pixel-identical so the handoff is invisible. Then the session state decides everything.

Slow-start threshold compressed from 3s to 1.8s and escalation from 8s to 4s in these demos; production uses the token values.

Composition

Sealed mark centred with the stacked lockup — mark above “Rukka Folio”, Folio at light weight, per §4.2 (splash and deck covers are the only sanctioned uses of the stacked lockup). Background is paper, not indigo — the app lives on paper, and the splash must not flash-cut. Dark mode inverts to ink background with the paper-book inverse mark (paper book, indigo strap and entries, bahi seal — the seal never changes colour); toggle the page to preview it live in the simulator. No tagline; splashes are quiet. Once inside the app, the wordmark disappears — the product speaks through the ledger, not the logo.

Branches

Session stateWhat playsTiming
Locked (auth gate ahead)Nothing. Mark stays sealed through splash and auth screen. Seal breaks only on successful unlock — never on the prompt.m = 0.6 on success
Session already openSealed → open, once, then hold open into home.m = 1 (~860ms)
Failed authStays sealed; horizontal shake ±2 units, 3 cycles.240ms
Slow cold startHold the sealed mark; past 3s, fade in the loader rule beneath with “Opening your books.” Loader leaves before the seal breaks.threshold 3s

For an end-to-end-encrypted product the locked branch is the common one — which means on most launches the splash animates nothing, and that is correct. If the splash played the seal breaking and a lock screen followed, the mark would be lying. Never add artificial delay to finish the animation for its own sake: if the app is ready, go — completing whichever beat is mid-flight, never cutting the seal-break halfway. Reduced motion: jump cuts throughout.

05

Rules & constants

Always

  • Loader is a rule: 2px hairline + ink segment
  • Counts over percentages when progress is known
  • Skeleton rows at true pitch — zero layout shift on swap
  • Loader waits 200ms before appearing
  • Phase 1 and 2 of splash are pixel-identical
  • Seal breaks only after a real unlock
  • Live-region announcements on every loader

Never

  • Spinners, anywhere
  • The mark as a loading animation
  • Shimmer or pulsing skeletons
  • Indigo or bahi in the loader — ink only
  • Artificial splash delay to “finish” the animation
  • Seal-break on the auth prompt (only on success)
  • Percentages where a count exists
