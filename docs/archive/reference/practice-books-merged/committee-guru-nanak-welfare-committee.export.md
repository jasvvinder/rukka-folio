# Day Book and Cash Book — Committee — Guru Nanak Welfare Committee

An export, not part of the printed book: what the app gives when the user downloads the Day Book and the Cash Book. The app lets the user choose any period and whether to include each entry's note; this export is the full year FY 2025-26, with notes. Entry numbers are the same as in the printed book's ledgers.

## Book: Guru Nanak Welfare Committee — committee book

### 1. Day Book — every entry of the year

In date order. Type: *Money in*, *Money out*, *Transfer* (between own cash, bank, card or deposit), *Gave on credit* / *Took on credit* (a khata entry with a person or shop — no money moved yet), *Advance settled* (an advance accounted for against the bills), *Paid by a partner* (a partner paid from his own pocket), *Paid by the joint family* (the family paid a household's bill), *Settled at year end* (a sub-family account squared for the year), *Correction* (an earlier entry put right) and *Fix / Adjust* (no money moved). An entry with more than one account on a side shows each account with its amount.
