# Committee — Guru Nanak Welfare Committee

**Guru Nanak Welfare Committee, Mohalla Baba Deep Singh Nagar, Phagwara** — a registered society run by its office-bearers. Forty member households pay ₹200 a month; the treasurer collects in cash and banks it. General donations are income; money given **for the community hall** goes to the hall fund and is spent only from it, and the fund's fixed deposit and its interest belong to it. The committee holds the Baisakhi mela, lights the gali for Diwali and Gurpurab, runs a medical camp, and pays a sanitation sewadar. Things bought are kept at what they cost.
