# Landlord — Gurdial Singh Sandhu, Jalandhar

**Gurdial Singh Sandhu** of Jalandhar lets three shops and a flat. Each tenant has a khata: the quarter's rent is written to it on the first day of the quarter, payments as they reach the bank. Deposits are money he owes back. One tenant leaves owing rent — deposit set off, part paid in cash, the rest written off. The caretaker holds a small float; surplus rent goes into a fixed deposit.
