Contractor — Bal Aluminium & PVC Works

Pradeep Bal fabricates and fits aluminium and uPVC windows and partitions. One builder pays a mobilisation advance, is billed in running bills and holds back 5% of each bill until the job is handed over — so part of the balance stays open and ages. Profiles come on credit; the glass dealer is paid in advance and then sends his bill. The site supervisor runs the labour money as an advance and accounts for it with labour slips; one float ends with ₹1,200 of lost slips.
