Truck operator — Sukhdev Singh Transport

Sukhdev Singh of Village Raipur, Tehsil Phillaur, District Jalandhar owns one truck and buys a second in December 2025 on a vehicle loan. Freight for two regular parties is written to their khata at the end of each quarter from the bilty book and collected the next month — one party pays late and in part. Diesel before each trip is filled at the home pump on a khata, settled quarterly. The driver carries a trip advance for road diesel, bhatta, hamali and tolls not on FASTag, and accounts for it against his trip sheets every quarter; tolls come off a FASTag wallet. The book is kept from the bank statement; the vehicle loan is kept in the book because this owner chooses to.
