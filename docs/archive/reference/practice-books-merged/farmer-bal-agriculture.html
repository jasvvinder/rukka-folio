Farmer — Bal Agriculture (Amar Bal)

Amar Bal farms wheat and basmati and keeps four buffaloes. The Kisan Credit Card is a loan account with the bank: money drawn on it, the bank's interest charged to it, and the crop money paid back into it. Fertiliser and sprays come on the dealer's khata; wheat is bought at MSP and paid straight into the bank; basmati is sold through an arhtiya who keeps his charges and pays the rest; milk goes to the dairy society and is paid every quarter here. A farm worker takes an advance against his wages.

WhoRole in this example
