# Contractor — Bal Aluminium & PVC Works

**Pradeep Bal** fabricates and fits aluminium and uPVC windows and partitions. One builder pays a mobilisation advance, is billed in running bills and holds back 5% of each bill until the job is handed over — so part of the balance stays open and ages. Profiles come on credit; the glass dealer is paid in advance and then sends his bill. The site supervisor runs the labour money as an advance and accounts for it with labour slips; one float ends with ₹1,200 of lost slips.

| Who | Role in this example |
|:---|:---|
| Pradeep Bal | Owner |
| Jagtar Singh | Site supervisor — holds the labour money as an advance, settled against labour slips |
| Sandhu Builders | Builder — mobilisation advance, running bills, 5% held back until hand-over |
| Mann Residency · Gill Residency | Customers — one job each |
| Alu-Tech Profiles | Supplier of profiles on khata |
| Glass Mart | Glass dealer — paid in advance, then sends his bill |

Amounts are in ₹; paise are shown only when there are paise. Plain words come first and the true side follows in small brackets — Money in (Dr), You will give (Cr) — so the side is never lost. Every figure is illustrative.
