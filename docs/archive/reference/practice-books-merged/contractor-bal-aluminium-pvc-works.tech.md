## Basis (ADR 2026-10-12 §2, §10–§12)

- No tax, GST, TDS, invoicing, stock or payroll (`docs/00-vision.md`, non-goals). Purchases for resale are an expense when bought or taken on credit; there is no closing stock.
- Transaction-driven: every entry is a bank or cash movement, a khata entry with a named party, a loan instalment as the bank paid it, a transfer between own accounts, an advance or its settlement, a partner's own-pocket payment, or a correction.
- No depreciation, prepaid, outstanding or closing entries; things owned stay at cost; the year's result (and a partnership's appropriation) is computed, never posted.
- Plain words in the printed book; the true side stays in a small bracket after the plain label (ADR 2026-10-10b §4). Ledger rows carry each entry's note in small type; the Day Book and the Cash Book are an export (`contractor-bal-aluminium-pvc-works.export.md`), not part of the printed book (ADR 2026-10-12 §11, amended 13 Oct 2026).
