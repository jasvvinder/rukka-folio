## How these were ruled — the product lens

The owner delegated the rulings on 20 Oct 2026 ("act as product architect … then close all"). Each row was decided against the same five facts, taken from `docs/00-vision.md` and the owner's earlier rulings, so that the answers form one system rather than 113 separate choices.

1. **Who keeps these books.** Indian households, joint families, their small businesses (shop, contractor, farm, truck, landlord, partnership) and trusts or committees — people who today keep a rokad, a khata and a ledger on paper. They are not accountants.
2. **What the books must give them.** Every entry recorded, typed by hand or taken from a bank statement; a live position every day; a statement for any account and any period; who owes what, and since when; each kind of book's year result; a clean carry-forward. In plain household words, with the true Dr/Cr in brackets.
3. **What is out of scope.** Tax, GST, TDS, invoicing, inventory and payroll; and any claim that the books comply with the Accounting Standards or a statutory format.
4. **One basis for every book — the khata basis** (O-01-1). Money is recorded when it moves; an amount due to or from a named person when it arises; nothing for the passage of time alone. Depreciation, accruals, prepayments and closing stock are an opt-in year-end step, never the default, and every report says which way it was kept.
5. **Where a source speaks, it wins** (in the approved hierarchy: ICAI pronouncements, then the textbooks, then bahi-khata practice). Where the sources are silent or split evenly, the ruling is the one that serves points 1–4, marked **P:** in the *Source* column with its reason. The owner's rulings of 12–13 Oct 2026 stand and are cited as such.

Rules that recur across many rows: one running account per person, with deposits, retention money, advances and interest-bearing loans in their own accounts on that person's page; corrections are new entries, never edits; anything that will not come back is *Written off* to an expense head; the year close is posted by the book itself, never typed by the user.

Row ids are `O-<topic>-<n>`. Book affected names the kind of book, never a person.
