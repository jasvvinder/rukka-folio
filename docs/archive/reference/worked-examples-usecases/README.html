Use-case worked examples — reading guide, class mapping and open readings (FY 2025-26)

Status: ⚠️ PROVISIONAL — pending bookkeeper sign-off. Seven use cases, 14 books, 1770 vouchers, one full financial year (01 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026), every month locked and every book closed with a certified vector. Everything here is generated by build.py from the voucher data in that one script and asserted to balance; the markdown, HTML, PDF, the technical companions and fixtures.json are outputs, never edited by hand. This set is written from the financial architecture specification and the ledger rules only (docs/reference/financial-accounting-standards.md, docs/02-ledger-rules.md, the ADRs named below) — not from the app's code and not from any demo data. It sits in docs/reference/worked-examples/usecases/, inside the one folder that holds every worked example (ADR 2026-10-12 §9); the original five files at that folder's root are untouched and remain the golden set until the owner rules otherwise (ADR 2026-10-11).

Two files per use case (ADR 2026-10-12 §2). <slug>.md (and its .html / .pdf) is the printed report — the real layout of the reports and statements a user prints or shares from the app, in the plain words a household or a shopkeeper writes in a diary or ledger book, with the true side in a small bracket after the plain label (Money in (Dr), You will get (Dr); ADR 2026-10-10b §4). Engine class names, verb ids, rule or ADR references, ⚠️ notes and generator checks never appear on it — build.py refuses to write a report that carries one (plain_lint), and check_md.py checks the same independently. <slug>.tech.md is the technical companion: per account the engine class, subtype, role and the standard and traditional types; per voucher the kind, refs, review and advance states; the rules the scenario exercises; the ⚠️ SPEC readings that apply; the checks; and the developer notes that used to sit in the report. The figures in the two files are the same figures.

Real names given by the owner are used here on purpose and are committed (ADR 2026-10-11 §2 reverses, for this folder only, the 4 Oct 2026 real names never committed note at .gitignore:41). Names only — no phone numbers, and every figure is illustrative.

Every printed book reads the way a ledger book is read, in the same order (owner ruling, 12 Oct 2026), under plain headings: (1) Accounts and opening balances — every account with its type word and its opening balance, Dr and Cr totals agreeing; (2) Account statements (ledgers) — every account, one after the other; an account with no entries still appears; then the supporting registers — (3) Day book with every entry's plain type (Money in, Money out, Gave on credit, Took on credit, Transfer, Correction / adjustment) and entered by, (4) Corrections and approvals, (5) Months closed and cash counts, (6) Advances, and the use-case section (bank statement, partners, tenants, gollak, retention, KCC); and at the end the closing statements: (7) Closing balances (trial balance), (8) Income and expenses for the year — the Income & Expenditure Account (trust) / Profit & Loss Account (business) / income and expenses (personal and family) with every head's total for the whole year, (9) What I have and what I owe (position) (07 §14 Full Position, by sign) with how the Corpus or the owner's funds are made up, and (10) Year end — balances carried to next year. The family file ends with the Family Reconciliation and the open advances across the family.

2. Period, openings, locks, close and layout

  • Period 01 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 (FY 2025-26); every book's start date is 01 Apr 2025 and its openings are dated there (ADR 2026-09-09d §4). Nothing is dated before the start; nothing is authored into a month after its lock (02 §8).
  • Openings: one adjustment per account — money, party, advance or partner — against Opening Balance / Capital, signed as the ledger sees it (02 §4; the engine's Verbs.openingBalance); a partner's contribution is Dr Opening Balance / Capital · Cr Partner Current; Opening Balance / Capital absorbs the difference and the chart shows it (ADR 2026-09-09c §4).
  • Locks: all twelve months, each lock dated in the following month; cash counted at close of business on the month's last day (02 §8 step 1), differences posted Dr/Cr Cash · Cr/Dr Adjustments; Suspense 0, no open flag and no pending advance as of every lock date (02 §8 step 3); aged advances warn only.
  • Year close (02 §8.1, ADR 2026-09-05e §2): the certified closing vector carries money, party, advance, partner and equity_system accounts as on 31 Mar 2026; category accounts are not carried; the year's surplus/deficit is one line; Accumulated surplus (business, family, personal) / Corpus (trust) is a computed line (02 §1.2); there is no closing journal entry; the vector becomes the FY 2026-27 b/f. The printed report calls the line Owner's funds / Partners' funds / Family's own funds in the business (accumulated surplus), Net worth or Corpus — the trust's own funds; fixtures.json keeps the 02 §1.2 name as its key.
  • Ledger layout (standards §2): Date · Entry · Particulars (To … on the debit side, By … on the credit side) · contextual Dr · contextual Cr · Balance · Side as its own column on every row; no minus signs. The opening row is drawn exactly as standards §2 draws it — Opening balance b/f · — · — · amount · side — and, being printed ledgers, they end Closing balance c/d · Total (incl. opening b/f) · Opening balance b/d with no separate c/f row (ADR 2026-09-24b §9: the opening row of an export is unchanged). Income and expense ledgers have no b/f row and end with Total for the year (ADR 2026-09-05e §2).
  • Vocabulary (ADR 2026-10-12 §2, ADR 2026-10-10b §4, 02 §10): the printed reports speak the user's language — Money in / Money out, You will get / You will give, Spent / Received, Cash in hand, Opening balance / Closing balance, Total for the year, What I have / What I owe — and every column header carries its true side in brackets, (Dr) or (Cr); the Side column and the trial balance show Dr/Cr plainly. The engine's posting logic never bends to the display language. Words 01 §1.3 forbids in the UI (voucher, narration, journal, contra, accrual) do not appear on a printed report — the day book says Entry and Details. Technical terms live in the technical companion and in this README. Amounts show paise only when non-zero; the engine holds integer paise throughout.

5. ⚠️ SPEC — readings taken where 02 and the ADRs are silent

Each is the most conservative reading available, flagged for the owner; none changes a locked line. Where a reading is overruled, build.py is changed and every file regenerated — the markdown is never patched. The kinds in S1–S3 coincide with the engine's own ⚠️ SPEC reading in packages/core_ledger/lib/src/invariants.dart (checked 11 Oct 2026); the generator's class-shape check mirrors that arm. Each technical companion repeats the readings its scenario exercises; the printed reports carry none of them.

  • S1 · kind of the advance postings. 02 §7 prescribes the postings (Dr Advance · Cr money; Dr expense · Cr Advance; Dr money · Cr Advance; write-off Dr Adjustments · Cr Advance) and ADR 2026-09-05e §6 calls the advance shape already reader-checked, but neither names the kind (02 §1.3 lists six). This set labels by nearest shape — request and spend money_out, return money_in, write-off adjustment — which is also how the engine's checkShape admits them.
  • S2 · kind of the partner postings. 02 §7.1's three events name postings, not kinds: own-pocket Dr Expense · Cr Partner Current is took_credit, takeout Dr Partner Current · Cr money is money_out (the engine treats partner as party-like), and the appropriation Dr Profit Distributed · Cr each Partner Current is one adjustment (exactly one equity_system counter-account, rule 7).
  • S3 · kinds of the one-sided inter-book case. 02 §6 calls a member pays a family expense from his own pocket the same mechanism as a transfer and ADR 2026-09-05e §6 admits the §6 pair shapes. The payer half Dr link · Cr Cash is a transfer; the payee half Dr Expense · Cr link is read as a money_out whose credit is the link — the engine's money_out arm admits a Due credit and its transfer arm admits only money and Due. Both halves share one transfer_group.
  • S4 · Suspense clearing. 02 §10 names the Suspense posting but not the clearing. This set clears with one guided adjustment Dr Suspense · Cr {counterpart} carrying the same refs.import_line (shape-legal under rule 7). The alternative — a refs.reverses mirror plus a fresh Money in — is also legal; owner to choose.
  • S5 · accounting date of an approved advance request. 02 §7 posts the request on approval and 02 §1.4 rule 4 makes entries immutable; this set keeps the request date as the accounting date (RA June: requested 10 Jun, approved 12 Jun, excluded from every balance in between, counted from 10 Jun once approved). If the owner prefers the approval date, only the ledger row's date moves.
  • S6 · ageing method. 02 §7 defines advance ageing as days since the oldest unsettled debit; 07 §7 gives party chips (> 30 amber, > 90 red) without a matching rule. This set reads both as FIFO: credits settle the oldest open debits first and an unapplied credit (deposit, prepayment) offsets the next debit. Consequence shown in Real Estate: a tenant's arrears only show as Dr once they exceed the deposit held.
  • S7 · the interest-on-capital setting. 02 §7.1 leaves the charging of a debit balance ⚠️ — charged at the same rate unless the setting says otherwise (ADR 2026-09-05e Open 2). The Bal Fabricators setting says credit balances only, chosen deliberately so that Harjeet's debit days neither earn nor are charged; the generator recomputes every interest figure from the daily balances under that setting and refuses any other. A different setting is a different, equally legal, example.
  • S8 · an owner who is not on the app. The Brick Field's structural_quorum is all_owners (chosen at creation, 02 §7.2.1); Nirmaljeet Gill holds no device, so no signed approval can exist for him and no structural action — profit distribution, interest, ratio change — can complete in-app. This set leaves the surplus undistributed and carried; the owner must rule how an off-app owner approves (a paper mandate recorded by an admin? a required invite?).
  • S9 · construction is expensed. 02 §1.2 has no fixed-asset class, so the langar hall's ₹5,50,000 reduces the year's result and the computed Corpus. Flagged, not solved (ADR 2026-10-12 §6 rules an asset class for a later build slice).
  • S10 · entry limits apply to every entry, counts included. 02 §3 flags any entry over the author's limit and 02 §7 says advance spends are approved per limits; a gollak count recorded by the sewadar (limit ₹5,000) would be flagged every month, so the Secretary records counts (the sheet still names the counter and the witness) and the sewadar's over-limit spends are flagged and approved the next day.
  • S11 · count timing. Cash is counted at close of business on the month's last day, any difference is dated that day, and the lock follows a few days later — so the certified month ends on counted cash (02 §8 step 1). A count performed on the lock day itself would date the adjustment in the next month; both are consistent with 02 §8.
  • S12 · link account naming. 02 §6 names the engine's accounts Due to/from {Book}; the owner ruled plain labels for this set. The class is unchanged (equity_system, role link) and the name carries the other book and its type, e.g. Randhawa Family Fund (joint family book).

Ruled by the owner (12 Oct 2026), no longer a reading: Manni Labour A/c and Ravinder Labour A/c are expense heads, not party accounts. Each labourer is paid his month's wages in cash on the month's last day (Dr Manni Labour · Cr Cash, a Money out), so nothing is owed at any month end and no party is needed; the year's total paid to each — ₹1,08,000 and ₹1,02,000 — is the Total for the year row of his ledger and his line in the trust's Income & Expenditure Account. Labour A/c stays for daily-wage labour at the big diwans. (Had wages been left unpaid at a month end, 02 §1.2 would need the person as a party to show what is owed.)

6. Known limits (avoided on purpose)

  • Receiving-book side of an owner's or partner's takeout. ADR 2026-09-09b fixes only the business side (Dr Drawings · Cr money); 02 §7.1 only the business side of a partner takeout. No book in this set records the receiving side: Jasvinder's household items are paid straight from the business account as Drawings and never enter his personal book (its P&L is therefore a deficit funded from savings — by construction); the Randhawa family takes nothing out of the Brick Field this year. Unruled; the owner should rule before a personal book is expected to show business takeouts as income.
  • Capital introduced from an asset never on the books. Jasvinder's ₹1,50,000 comes from a fixed deposit that matured straight into the business account; the FD predates 1 Apr 2025 and was never on the personal book (ADR 2026-09-09d §4 — the books begin at the start date), so no personal-side posting is needed.
  • Sood (interest an arhtiya charges on an advance) is not posted — open roadmap row (10, Interest on hand loans). Sodhi & Sons' ₹60,000 advance is settled by the next sale without interest.
  • No EMI with principal and interest in one bank line (the KCC bank debits interest separately); no party-to-party posting; no credit to an expense category outside adjustment/reversal; no write-off to P&L (write-offs go to Adjustments; ADR 2026-10-12 §8 rules a Written off head for a later slice); no fixed assets (kilns, land, cattle, the langar hall are not on the books; ADR 2026-10-12 §6 rules an asset class for a later slice); no GST or TDS (every rent is below ₹2,40,000 a year; no TDS on contract bills modelled); no late arrival, no re-opened month or year, no loss sharing, no route-1/route-2 partner settlement posting; INR only.
  • The joint fund's P&L is a deficit by construction: business surplus reaches it through 02 §6 pairs, never through an income account (02 §7.1 last paragraph), so its own P&L shows only the common spending while the links show what each business has remitted.
  • Two-member books flag only the limited member's entries; admins carry no limit, so the admin-held-to-a-lower-threshold option (02 §7.2 item 2, default ⚠️) is not exercised.

8. Regenerating, checking, fixtures, and what would make this set golden

  • python3 build.py in this folder regenerates every file — the seven printed reports (.md, .html, .pdf), the seven technical companions (.tech.md), this README and fixtures.json. --no-pdf skips Chrome; --chrome PATH points at another Chrome; --self-test runs the mutation checks below. The script uses the standard library only, integer paise everywhere, no clock, no randomness, relative paths; Chrome's /CreationDate and /ModDate are pinned so the PDFs are byte-stable.
  • What build.py asserts on every run, and refuses to write otherwise: every voucher sums to zero with ≥ 2 non-zero lines; every account belongs to its book; the class-shape of every kind (02 §1.4 rule 7 as the engine's checkShape reads it, incl. the advance, partner and §6 shapes); every trial balance balances; every inter-book pair has two halves of the same date and amount whose link lines mirror, and every pair nets to 0; Opening Balance / Capital exactly once per book; Drawings only in a one-owner business book and partner accounts only in a shared one; capital introduced is Dr money · Cr Opening Balance / Capital and never income (ADR 2026-09-13 §4); an owner takeout is Dr Drawings · Cr money and never an expense (ADR 2026-09-09b §3); nothing leaves a collection box except a Transfer to cash or bank and nothing enters it except a count, whose counted by and witness are two different people (02 §8.2); nothing is dated before the start and nothing is authored into a locked month or before its own accounting date; an entry over its author's limit carries a review flag decided by a different admin/head before the lock, a rejected one has its mirror reversal, and only an admin or head amends or reverses (06 §1.0); an advance request is approved by a different admin/head before the lock and no advance account goes negative; Suspense is 0, no flag is open and no advance is pending as of every lock date; every cash account is counted in every month before its lock (trust: with a denomination sheet that sums to the count); the appropriation is one entry whose interest lines equal an independent recomputation (half-up to the paisa, credit days only, Actual/365) and whose share lines equal the floor-by-weight split with the remainder on the largest weight (ties to the earliest-created account); the closing vector carries only balance-sheet classes and sums to zero with the net line; no cash, collection, savings or current account is negative after any posting; and the printed report carries no engine class, verb id, rule or ADR reference, ⚠️ note, backtick or generator check (plain_lint, ADR 2026-10-12 §2).
  • Mutation check — python3 build.py --self-test rebuilds the set in memory, changes one guarded posting at a time (the remainder moved to the wrong partner; an interest line a paisa short; an interest figure not from credit-day balances; capital introduced credited to income; an owner takeout debited to an expense; a gollak count whose witness is the counter; a Transfer into the gollak; an operator amending; an entry authored before its date; the pocket-expense half labelled transfer; a Suspense line left uncleared; cash driven negative) and reports whether the validator refused each one. All refusals are expected; a not refused line is a defect in the generator.
  • Independent re-derivation — check_md.py (committed beside the generator, standard library only, no code shared with build.py) parses the printed markdown as a reader would: it sums every day book, rebuilds every trial balance, re-runs every account statement's running balance and khata block, re-adds every year-end carried vector, checks that every book runs accounts → statements → closing balances → income and expenses → position → year end, that the accounts table lists exactly the accounts of the statements and the trial balance with openings that total Dr = Cr and match each statement's b/f row, that every head's year total in the income and expense statement equals its statement and the statement balances, that the position's has − owes equals the computed Corpus / owner's funds and its make-up, and that no technical token (§, ADR, backtick, ⚠️, engine class, verb id, voucher) appears in print. build.py runs it after writing the seven printed reports and stops on any difference; this build passed 6794 checks with 0 differences. Run it by hand with python3 -I check_md.py in this folder.
  • fixtures.json holds every book (type, ownership, members with limits), every account (class, subtype, role, link, opening paise), every voucher (kind, accounting date, authored date, the narration as stored — with its rule citation — signed lines in paise with the interest/share line tags, refs, review and advance states, count sheets), the locks, the cash counts with denominations, and the expected closing balances, TB totals and year-close vectors — so an engine golden test can consume this set without parsing markdown. The plain-language rework of 12 Oct 2026 changed no figure and no field of it except content_hash_md.
