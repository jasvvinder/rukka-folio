# Trust — Radha Soami Satsang Sabha

**About this book:** Organization (trust) book — gollak, langar, dairy, construction, two labourers on monthly wages. A satsang sabha with a monthly gollak count, sponsor donations, a langar, a small dairy run on donations, a new langar hall under construction, two labourers paid monthly wages in cash — each with his own expense head — and daily-wage labour at the big diwans, and a sewadar who takes langar advances. The gollak is a collection box: a count is income, with a note-count sheet, a counter and a witness; it is emptied **only** by Transfer into the Cash A/c or the bank; nothing is ever paid from it. Every trust cash count — gollak or cash — has a note-count sheet.

**Period:** 01 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 (FY 2025-26) · every month closed · year closed. Sample report — the figures are illustrative.

| Person | Role in this example |
|:---|:---|
| Ranjeet Singh | Secretary · admin, no entry limit · witness at every gollak count · approves the sewadar's advances and over-limit entries |
| Amritpal Singh | Sewadar · member, entry limit ₹5,000 · counts the gollak and the cash · holds langar advances |
| Manni · Ravinder | labourers on monthly wages, paid in cash at each month end — each has his own **expense head** (*Manni Labour A/c*, *Ravinder Labour A/c*), so the year's total paid to each shows in his statement and in the income and expenses for the year. Nothing is owed to them at any month end, so no person account is needed |
| Daily-wage labourers | casual labour hired for the big diwans and repairs — booked to the general *Labour A/c* |

How to read it: amounts are in ₹, with paise shown only when they are not zero. Every statement is written the way a ledger book is, with the plain meaning first and the true side in brackets — Money in (Dr), Money out (Cr), You will get (Dr), You will give (Cr). An account statement runs Date · Entry · Particulars (*To …* on the Dr side, *By …* on the Cr side) · Dr · Cr · Balance · Side; it opens with the balance brought forward and ends with the closing balance carried down, the totals and the opening balance for the new year. Income and expense heads have no opening balance and end with their total for the year.

## Book: Radha Soami Satsang Sabha — Trust Book

Trust book · started 01 Apr 2025 · members: Ranjeet Singh — admin (Secretary), no limit; Amritpal Singh — member (Sewadar), entry limit ₹5,000.

**Construction A/c is an expense:** the langar hall's cost reduces the year's result and the Corpus instead of appearing as a thing owned — a known limit.

The gollak: each count is entered as donation income for the full counted amount, with the note-count sheet, *counted by* and *witness*; the box is then emptied by Transfer — part into the Cash A/c the same day, the rest into the bank two days later — so the Gollak balance is always exactly what is still in the box and is nil before the next count. No expense and no advance is ever paid from it.
