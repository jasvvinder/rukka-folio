Trust — Radha Soami Satsang Sabha

About this book: Organization (trust) book — gollak, langar, dairy, construction, two labourers on monthly wages. A satsang sabha with a monthly gollak count, sponsor donations, a langar, a small dairy run on donations, a new langar hall under construction, two labourers paid monthly wages in cash — each with his own expense head — and daily-wage labour at the big diwans, and a sewadar who takes langar advances. The gollak is a collection box: a count is income, with a note-count sheet, a counter and a witness; it is emptied only by Transfer into the Cash A/c or the bank; nothing is ever paid from it. Every trust cash count — gollak or cash — has a note-count sheet.

Period: 01 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 (FY 2025-26) · every month closed · year closed. Sample report — the figures are illustrative.

How to read it: amounts are in ₹, with paise shown only when they are not zero. Every statement is written the way a ledger book is, with the plain meaning first and the true side in brackets — Money in (Dr), Money out (Cr), You will get (Dr), You will give (Cr). An account statement runs Date · Entry · Particulars (To … on the Dr side, By … on the Cr side) · Dr · Cr · Balance · Side; it opens with the balance brought forward and ends with the closing balance carried down, the totals and the opening balance for the new year. Income and expense heads have no opening balance and end with their total for the year.
