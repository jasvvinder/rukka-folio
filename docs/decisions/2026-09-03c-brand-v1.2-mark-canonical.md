# ADR 2026-09-03c — The v1.2 brand asset drop is canonical for the mark; numerals stay Mukta

The designer delivered Brand Guidelines v1.2 (`docs/brand/`): the text, the rendered visual
guidelines, the mark animation reference page, the master SVGs and a full icon package
(v1.2.3). Reviewing them against the repo, the owner ruled: "the current files are latest.
Update all with the new one."

## Rulings 🔒

1. **Mark geometry is the v1.2 drop.** The strap is inset 2 units top and bottom (x28 w14,
   y14–74), so a hairline of book keeps the silhouette closed on paper grounds. Everything
   else (book, seal, entries, open state, margins, timings) is unchanged. `docs/brand/icons/master/*.svg`
   is the geometry of record; `docs/brand/rukka-folio-mark-animation.html` is the acceptance
   reference for the animation; `docs/brand/icons/` is the shipped icon package.
2. **The mark is embedded, never redrawn.** Screens carry the master SVG (light: indigo book;
   dark: paper-book inverse with indigo strap and entries; seal stays bahi red `#C1502E` in
   both — never the lifted dark accent). Placeholder line-art marks are defects.
3. **Amount numerals are Mukta with tabular figures, not a monospace face.** The visual
   guidelines page had set its amount rows and numerals specimen in IBM Plex Mono, contradicting
   11 §4.4's one-family rule; the page is corrected. Mukta and Mukta Mahee both ship `tnum`
   (tabular digits 510 units at Regular), so Latin digits align across EN/हिन्दी/ਪੰਜਾਬੀ screens.
   Monospace is reserved for OTP/PIN boxes, codes and raw bank text.

## Not ruled here

Loader/waiting pattern and splash treatment — the owner will decide separately. Nothing is
built for them yet (design-system §6, brand README).
