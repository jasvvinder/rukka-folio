# ADR 2026-09-03b — The doors into Move money and the adjustment wizards

Follow-on to ADR 2026-09-03 (Menu rows). Reviewing canvas 2 the owner accepted two gaps and
said "Okay, Close them."

## Rulings 🔒

1. **Move money is the fifth position of the entry screen's verb pill** — Money in · Money
   out · Gave on credit · Took on credit · **Move money**. The pill already switches by swipe
   or tap without losing the amount (07 §entry); the transfer (S2.3) is one more swipe, not a
   separate button, tile or Menu row. Home keeps its four verb tiles.
2. **Each adjustment wizard (S2.4) has exactly one door, on the screen where its problem
   shows:** cash count difference ← the cash count sheet (S5.5, on *Save count* when the
   figures differ) · write off a balance ← the khata's A/C statement ⋯ menu (S4; Admin & Head)
   · fix an old entry ← the locked entry's *Fix this entry* (S4.1) · opening balance correction
   ← **Settings → Your books → Opening balances** (S13, new row).

## What changed where

- **07 §entry** — pill positions sentence added. **13 §screen table** — S2.3 and S2.4 parents.
- **Design** — canvas 2: every S2 pager shows five dots; row 5 opens from S2 with the swipe;
  the wizards row is a branch with *opens from* cards per wizard. Canvas 10 S13: *Opening
  balances* row under *Your books*. The S4 ⋯ menu's "Write off this balance" item is named
  on the canvas but the menu sheet itself is not drawn (design gap, not a rule gap).
